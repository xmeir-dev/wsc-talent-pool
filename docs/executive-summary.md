# Executive summary

Conference contacts get lost in spreadsheets. This tool brings them into one pool and explains whom to review for an open role, including WSC colleagues who may know them. For Senior ML Engineer, its rules shortlist 6 of 75 people, flag 6 for review and mark 63 not recommended for that role. Those are rule outputs, not validated hiring judgments; [known gaps](scoring.md#limits) remain. The recruiter view works; HubSpot and Comeet integration is simulated. Screening savings and hiring impact have not been measured.
