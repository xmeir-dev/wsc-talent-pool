# Executive summary

Our recruiters meet strong people at conferences, but those contacts end up in scattered spreadsheets and are forgotten when a matching role opens, and there are too many to screen by hand. This tool keeps every conference contact in one pool and, when a role opens, sorts them for that role. For the Senior ML Engineer role, the tool set aside 63 of the 75 conference contacts as not relevant to this role, flagged 6 for a recruiter to check, and shortlisted 6, each with the reasons in plain words and the names of WSC colleagues who may know them. Recruiters work the pool in one simple view, and in a production version the same results would land in HubSpot the morning after a conference, with a handoff to Comeet for each role. The integration demo simulates this; it is not connected to either product. It narrows the list; whether it saves time or leads to more hires still has to be measured.
