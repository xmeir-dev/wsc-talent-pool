# Executive summary

Our recruiters meet strong people at conferences, but those contacts end up in scattered spreadsheets and are forgotten when a matching role opens, and there are too many to screen by hand. This tool keeps every conference contact in one pool and, when a role opens, sorts them for that role. For the Senior ML Engineer role, the tool set aside 63 of the 75 conference contacts as not relevant to this role, flagged 5 for a recruiter to check, and shortlisted 7, each with the reasons in plain words and the names of WSC colleagues who may know them. In daily use, recruiters would not open a new tool: the morning after a conference, HubSpot tells them who is worth a look for each open role, and one click hands a person into Comeet. It narrows the list; whether it saves time or leads to more hires still has to be measured.
