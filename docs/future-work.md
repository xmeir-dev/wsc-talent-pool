# Future work: beyond conferences

**Parked ideas, 2026-10-19. Not built or required for this assignment.** Captured from the discussion with Meir; access and value need confirming with WSC.

## Possible use cases

| Source | Opportunity and limit |
|---|---|
| Past strong candidates | Revisit permitted Comeet feedback when a similar role opens. |
| Employee referrals | Match recommendations made before a suitable opening existed. |
| People who declined | Reconnect after a relevant change, respecting their preferences. |
| Former employees, interns and contractors | Consider updated experience using approved records. |
| General expressions of interest | Match résumés and future-opportunities sign-ups to later roles. |
| Layoff outreach | Use volunteered availability or opt-in lists. A company announcement does not identify affected individuals. |

## Suggested first extension

**Past strong candidates:** earlier interviews may offer useful evidence. First confirm access and whether the feedback remains relevant. This is a proposed priority, not a build commitment.

## Keep the same principles

Use HubSpot and Comeet where possible. Preserve each source and date; a referral or past relationship proves neither qualification nor interest. Keep one person’s history across roles, avoid duplicate outreach, and respect declines and active processes. Confirm current information and contact preferences.
