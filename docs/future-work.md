# Future work: beyond conferences

**Status: parked ideas, 2026-10-19. Not part of the current build or assignment scope.** Captured from the discussion with Meir; no implementation is requested yet.

The opportunity: when a role opens, surface relevant people from a shared talent pool, with their previous relationship to WSC and contact history visible.

## Possible use cases

These are proposed sources; availability and access need confirming with WSC.

| Use case | When it helps | Possible source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Past strong candidates** | Someone interviewed well but another person got the offer. A similar role opens and they resurface with the previous feedback. | Comeet application history and permitted interview feedback |
| **Employee referrals** | An employee recommends someone before a suitable opening exists. A later role matches them, with the referrer visible. | Employee submissions and existing referral records |
| **People who previously declined** | Timing, location or the role did not work before. A relevant change gives a reason to reconnect, respecting contact preferences. | Previous conversations and role-specific outcomes |
| **Former employees, interns and contractors** | Someone with a previous WSC relationship has since gained experience relevant to an opening. | Approved internal records and updated information from the person |
| **General expressions of interest** | Someone sends a résumé or joins a future-opportunities list before a matching role exists. | Recruiting inbox submissions or a talent-community form |
| **Layoff outreach** | People affected by another company's layoffs indicate they are available and match an open role. | People volunteering their availability or an opt-in talent list; a company announcement alone does not identify who was affected |

## Suggested first extension

**Past strong candidates.** Previous interviews may provide richer evidence than conference registration. First establish which history WSC can access and whether it is still relevant. This is a suggested priority, not a commitment to build.

## Keep the same principles

- **Work inside existing tools.** Prefer HubSpot and Comeet; add a dashboard only for an essential task they cannot support well.
- **Assess fit separately from source.** A referral, former WSC relationship or layoff does not itself establish qualification or interest. Preserve where the information came from and when it was recorded.
- **Keep one person with history per role.** Combine sources without duplicate outreach; preserve declines, active recruitment and contact restrictions. See [contact tracking](additional-info/contact-tracking-plan.md).
- **Confirm current information and respect contact preferences.** Missing availability stays unknown. Apply the existing [data and privacy assumptions](assumptions.md#privacy).
